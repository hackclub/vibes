Why Vibes?

This program is not about AI taking over or skipping the hard parts of learning. It is about using AI as a tool.

For beginners, making a website live involves more than HTML and CSS. GitHub, repositories, files, and deployment can all feel overwhelming. Vibes keeps the focus on the basics while still letting people experiment with established code.

Meeting Requirements

    - Computer and Internet
    - VSCode or any other IDE
    - Access to a chatbot (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini work perfectly)
    - A GitHub account!
