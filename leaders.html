Why Vibes?

This program isn't about AI taking over or skipping the hard parts of learning. It's about using AI as a tool.

For beginners, making a website live involves more than HTML and CSS. GitHub, repositories, files, and deployment can all feel overwhelming. Vibes keeps the focus on the basics while still letting people experiment with established code.

Meeting Requirements

  • Computer and internet
  • Hack Club Spaces (or another IDE)
  • Access to a chatbot (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini all work great)
  • A GitHub account!

How do I get coins?

When you're ready to wrap up the workshop, navigate to the Clubs Platform and an admin will close you out.
